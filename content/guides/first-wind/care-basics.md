A little care goes a long way.

## Winding

- **Manual-wind:** wind at the same time every day until you feel resistance, then stop. Never force it.
- **Automatic:** if it has stopped, give the crown 20–30 turns to start it, then wear it.
- Take the watch **off your wrist** to wind it, to avoid stressing the crown stem.

## Setting the date safely

Many mechanical watches should not have their date changed between roughly **9 pm and 3 am**, because the date mechanism is engaged then. If in doubt, move the hands to 6:30 first, then set the date.

## Water

- Push in or screw down the crown before getting wet.
- Rinse with fresh water after the sea or a pool.
- Have water resistance checked every year or two if you swim with the watch.

## Magnets

Phones, speakers, laptop lids and bag clasps can magnetise a mechanical watch and make it run very fast. A watchmaker can demagnetise it in seconds, or you can use an inexpensive demagnetiser.

## Cleaning and storage

- Clean bracelets with a soft toothbrush and warm, soapy water (only if the watch is water-resistant).
- Store watches away from direct sunlight and magnetic fields, ideally in a watch box.
- **Quartz:** replace a dead battery promptly, because old batteries can leak.
- **Mechanical:** plan a service every 5–10 years, see [Servicing](../../automatic/servicing/).
