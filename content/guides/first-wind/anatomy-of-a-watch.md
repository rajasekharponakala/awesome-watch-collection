Before you can compare watches, you need the words for what you are looking at. A wristwatch is made of a few main groups of parts.

## The case

- **Case**: the metal (or resin, ceramic, titanium) housing that protects the movement.
- **Lugs**: the "horns" at the top and bottom that hold the strap or bracelet.
- **Crown**: the knob used to wind the watch and set the time. A **screw-down crown** seals the case for better water resistance.
- **Pushers**: extra buttons, usually for a chronograph (stopwatch).
- **Caseback**: the back of the case. It is either solid or an **exhibition caseback** in sapphire that shows the movement.
- **Crown guards**: shoulders on the case that protect the crown from knocks.

## The face

- **Crystal**: the transparent cover. It is made of **acrylic** (warm, scratches but polishes out), **mineral glass** (cheap and fairly tough) or **sapphire** (very scratch-resistant).
- **Bezel**: the ring around the crystal. It may be fixed, or rotate for timing dives, tracking a second time zone or calculating speed.
- **Dial**: the face itself, with **indices** (hour markers), a **minute track** and often text.
- **Hands**: hour, minute and seconds. Their shapes have names: *dauphine*, *sword*, *Mercedes*, *baton*, *cathedral*.
- **Lume**: glow-in-the-dark material on the hands and markers.

## The inside

- **Movement** (or **calibre**): the engine. It can be mechanical, quartz or electronic.
- **Rotor**: in an automatic watch, the weight that winds the mainspring as you move.

## The strap

- **Strap** (leather, rubber, fabric) or **bracelet** (metal links), joined to the lugs with **spring bars** and closed with a **buckle** or **clasp**.
