## The four main channels

| Channel | Price | Warranty | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authorised dealer (AD) / boutique** | List price (sometimes discounted) | Full manufacturer warranty | Lowest |
| **Grey market** | Often below list for most brands; above list for hyped models | Dealer warranty, sometimes manufacturer | Low to medium |
| **Pre-owned dealer** | Usually below new, depending on the model | Dealer warranty, typically 1–2 years | Medium |
| **Private sale / forums / auction** | Potentially the best deals | None, or limited | Highest |

## Protecting yourself

1. **Buy the seller before the watch.** Check reviews, history and returns policy.
2. **Pay with protection:** a credit card or escrow, never a bank transfer to a stranger.
3. **"Box and papers"** (original box, warranty card, receipts) add value and confidence, but papers can be faked too.
4. **Ask for a timegrapher reading** and recent service records.
5. **Compare serial and reference numbers** with known production data.
6. **If it's too cheap, it's fake or stolen.** No one sells a desirable watch for half of its market value.

## Depreciation and hype

Most new watches lose value when they leave the shop. A few models trade above list price because of scarcity. That premium can vanish, as many buyers learned after the 2022 market correction. **Buy what you love, not what you think will appreciate.**
