A **complication** is any function beyond hours, minutes and seconds.

## Chronograph

A stopwatch built into the watch. Start and stop with the top pusher, reset with the bottom one.

- **Cam-lever** (e.g. ETA 7750): robust and economical; pushers can feel firm.
- **Column-wheel**: a castellated wheel coordinates the functions, with a smoother feel; prized by connoisseurs.
- **Horizontal vs vertical clutch:** a vertical clutch avoids the seconds hand jumping at start.
- **Flyback:** press reset while running and the chronograph instantly restarts. Made for pilots.
- **Rattrapante (split-seconds):** two seconds hands to time two events. One of the most difficult complications to make.

## GMT and world time

- **"Flyer" or "true" GMT:** the local hour hand jumps independently, ideal for travellers.
- **"Caller" or "office" GMT:** the 24-hour hand is set independently, ideal for following another time zone from home.
- **World time:** a city ring and a 24-hour ring show all time zones at once (Louis Cottier's 1930s system).

## Calendars

| Complication | Corrections needed |
|---|---|
| Simple date | 5 per year (every 30-day month and February) |
| Annual calendar | 1 per year (end of February) |
| Perpetual calendar | None until 2100 (not a leap year in the Gregorian calendar) |

## Moon phase

A disc with two moons turns once every two lunar months. A standard moon phase is out by one day after about **2.5 years**. "Astronomical" versions stay accurate for over 100 years.

## Others

**Power-reserve indicator**, **alarm** (mechanical alarms buzz with a small hammer), **tachymeter** scales and **regatta** countdown timers.
