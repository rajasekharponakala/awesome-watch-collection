Exotic watches are where horology becomes art. Some are historical dead ends, like the tuning-fork watch overtaken by quartz, and some are modern experiments in how we read time.

## Collecting exotics

- **Know who can service it.** Tuning-fork and early electronic watches need specialists.
- **Documentation matters even more** for independents and low-production pieces.
- **Wear before you buy.** Unusual displays can be thrilling or frustrating in daily life.
