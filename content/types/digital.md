In the early 1970s the digital watch was the height of luxury: the first Pulsar cost more than a car. Within a decade, LCD technology had made digital watches the cheapest timekeepers ever made.

## Why digital matters to collectors

Digital watches are design and technology history you can actually wear. The **Casio F-91W**, the **G-Shock**, the **Seiko** "James Bond" LCD watches and the **Hamilton Pulsar** are all collectible, and many cost very little.

## Buying tips

- Check the **module number** on the caseback. It tells you exactly which functions the watch has.
- Look for **solar** and **radio-controlled** versions if you never want to set or change a battery.
- For tough use, the **G-Shock** standard of shock resistance is the benchmark.
