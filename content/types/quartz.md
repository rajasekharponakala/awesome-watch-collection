When Seiko sold the first quartz wristwatch on Christmas Day 1969, it began the **quartz crisis**: within fifteen years, cheap and astonishingly accurate quartz watches had devastated the traditional Swiss industry.

## How it works

A battery sends current through a tiny tuning-fork-shaped quartz crystal. Quartz is *piezoelectric*: it vibrates at a precise frequency when electrified. An integrated circuit counts 32,768 vibrations and sends one pulse per second to a stepper motor that moves the hands.

## Why choose quartz

- **Accuracy** that the best mechanical watches cannot match
- **Grab-and-go**: no winding, no setting after a weekend off the wrist
- **Thin and light** cases
- **Cost**: superb quartz watches exist at every price, up to Grand Seiko 9F and beyond

Quartz is not a lesser choice, it is a different one. Many collectors keep at least one excellent quartz piece in the box.
