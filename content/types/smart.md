Smartwatches are now the best-selling category of watch in the world by volume. For collectors they raise new questions: **how long will it be supported?**, **which ecosystem am I locked into?** and **what happens to my health data?**

## Choosing a smartwatch

1. **Match your phone.** Apple Watch only works with iPhone; most others work best with Android.
2. **Decide what matters: battery or apps.** Full-OS watches charge daily; outdoor watches can last weeks.
3. **Check sensor accuracy** for the sports you do. Wrist heart rate is weaker for intervals and weightlifting.
4. **Think about longevity.** A mechanical watch lasts generations; a smartwatch typically lasts a few years.

## Smartwatches and traditional watches

Many enthusiasts wear both: a smartwatch for training, a mechanical watch for everything else. Hybrid watches try to combine the two, see the Hybrid section.
