Hybrid watches resist easy classification, and that is exactly what makes them fascinating.

## Spring Drive in one paragraph

The mainspring unwinds through a normal gear train, but instead of an escapement, the final wheel, the **glide wheel**, spins continuously. As it spins it generates a small current that powers a quartz crystal and an integrated circuit. The circuit compares the glide wheel's speed with the crystal and applies a precise **electromagnetic brake**. There is no tick, just a seconds hand that flows like time itself.

## Hybrid smartwatches

Brands such as Withings and Garmin hide activity tracking inside classic-looking cases. They suit people who want health data without a glowing screen.
